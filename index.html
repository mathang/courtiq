🏀 How CourtIQ Works

  1. 1. Position on the Half-Court (15s Movement Clock): Move with WASD or Arrows; hold Shift for Turbo. There are two action keys: Space shoots, or automatically attempts a dunk when you are moving towards the basket inside the blue paint. Dunk styles are random: tomahawk, windmill, 360 or reverse. Tap C within half a second: 1 tap = crossover, 2 = spin, 3 = eurostep, 4+ = stepback. Moves cost stamina. On defence, C swipes for a steal; a missed swipe freezes you for 1 second. Space jumps to block; hold it during a dunk take-off for a forgiving swat attempt from within 2.6m. The jump faces the attacker automatically. Press F for fullscreen. Inside the arc scores 2 points; outside scores 3.
  2. 2. Release the Shot (Space) & Slow-Motion Flight: When the ball leaves the attacker's hands, court positions and block eligibility (≤2.6m and facing shooter) are frozen, and the ball enters a suspended slow-motion arc. On a dunk, you take off and hang in the air for the question before the finish resolves. A timed physical swat overrides the basket; both students still answer and receive learning credit. A clean steal changes possession immediately and is excluded from question accuracy.
  3. 3. Protected Reading & Single-Lock Answer Duel: After the protected reading period (at least 2 seconds), both players answer the same multiple-choice question (1–4 or tap). Each player gets one locked submission. Option order is shuffled independently per device.
  4. 4. Knowledge + Speed + Positioning = Outcome:
    • Attacker correct first (or tied within 200ms): Earns up to +70% accuracy bonus based on speed: \(P = \text{clamp}(B + C(0.35 + 0.35S), 0.03, 0.95)\).
    • Eligible defender (≤2.6m) correct first: Swats and BLOCKS the shot!
    • Defender correct first but out of range: Credited in learning accuracy, but too far away to block.
  5. 5. Learn, Then Continue: Your feedback says whether you were correct, shows your chosen answer, and explains the specific misconception if you were wrong. Read the correct reasoning and course source, then press Continue. In student-versus-student matches, both students must continue. Three correct questions in a row ignites “ON FIRE!”: move 20% faster and gain +20 percentage points to shooting, capped at 95%. Fire lasts until a wrong or unanswered question. Misses and blocks do not break your learning streak.
  6. 6. Alternating Possessions & Equal Opportunity: Possession switches after every attempt. First to 11 points (max 10 possessions each), always preserving equal possessions and paired overtime rounds.
