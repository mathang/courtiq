🏀 How CourtIQ Works

  1. 1. Position on the Half-Court (15s Movement Clock): Move with WASD or Arrows. Hold Shift for Turbo. Use C to cross over, Q to spin, E to eurostep, and X to step back. Hold a direction to choose a side. Moves cost stamina and recharge briefly. Press R within 3.4m for an arcade dunk. Press V to choose tomahawk, windmill, 360 or reverse (styles also cycle after each shot). Defend with Z to swipe: a miss freezes you for 1 second. Press B or Space during the dunk take-off to jump and swat it. Stay close, face the attacker and time your leap; early or late jumps miss. Press F for fullscreen. Inside the arc scores 2 points (Close base 20%, Mid-range base 12%); outside the 6.75m arc scores 3 points (base 7%).
  2. 2. Release the Shot (Space) & Slow-Motion Flight: When the ball leaves the attacker's hands, court positions and block eligibility (≤1.8m and facing shooter) are frozen, and the ball enters a suspended slow-motion arc. On a dunk, you take off and hang in the air for the question before the finish resolves. A timed physical swat overrides the basket; both students still answer and receive learning credit. A clean steal changes possession immediately and is excluded from question accuracy.
  3. 3. Protected Reading & Single-Lock Answer Duel: After a 2-second reading period, both players answer the same multiple-choice question (1–4 or tap). Each player gets one locked submission. Option order is shuffled independently per device.
  4. 4. Knowledge + Speed + Positioning = Outcome:
    • Attacker correct first (or tied within 200ms): Earns up to +70% accuracy bonus based on speed: \(P = \text{clamp}(B + C(0.35 + 0.35S), 0.03, 0.95)\).
    • Eligible defender (≤1.8m) correct first: Swats and BLOCKS the shot!
    • Defender correct first but out of range: Credited in learning accuracy, but too far away to block.
  5. 5. Alternating Possessions & Equal Opportunity: Possession switches after every attempt. First to 11 points (max 10 possessions each), always preserving equal possessions and paired overtime rounds.
