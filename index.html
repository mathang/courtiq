🏀 How CourtIQ Works

  • WASD or joystick to move. Hold L for turbo.
  • J to shoot. Drive into the blue paint for a dunk. On defence, hold J as the shooter takes off to try a block.
  • K for a trick or to steal on defence. Missing a steal costs a second.
    Trick movesTap K in quick succession. One tap crosses over, two spin, three eurostep and four step back.
  • Look for IF CORRECT on the floor. It estimates your chance of scoring with a correct answer halfway through answer time and no block. More space helps.
  • Answer with 1–4 or tap. A faster correct answer boosts a shot or can block it if you’re close enough on defence. Read the feedback, then choose Continue.
  • Three correct answers in a row puts you On Fire for a speed and shooting boost.
